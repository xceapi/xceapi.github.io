About me

I've loved travelling since I was a kid, and showing people around my city is the part I enjoy most.

Now I study Tourism at the Faculty of Geography (University of Bucharest), and I have a Level 4 Tourism Technician certificate, the highest level for that qualification here. I speak English and Romanian.

I travel a lot myself too, so I know how it feels to land somewhere new and have no idea where anything is. That's pretty much why I started this.

When I'm not doing any of this I'm usually out for a walk at night, at the gym, or doing some calisthenics in the park. I also go to electronic music nights sometimes, so if you want club tips, ask.
